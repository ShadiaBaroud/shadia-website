Biography & Experience

Systems Engineering Grounding Meets Advanced AI Research

My background spans 9 years as a database developer and systems analyst before transitioning into full-time AI research. This provides my technical framework designs with a robust systems-engineering foundation rather than a purely theoretical one.

As an IEEE Senior Member and two-time ITU AI for Good Scholar (Geneva), my research targets privacy-preserving federated learning, multi-agent systems (MAS), and generative models under uncertainty. I maintain active international collaborations, including work with the University of Glasgow (EPSRC TransiT) on sustainable transport decarbonization.
